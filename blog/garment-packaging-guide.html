Whether you run a clothing brand, a garment factory, or an online store, packaging is part of your product. Customers see it before they see the garment — and increasingly, they judge you by it. This guide covers the full packaging ecosystem: hangtags, labels, and bags, and how to choose them as a system.

Why Garment Packaging Matters

  • First impression — 70% of purchase decisions are made at the point of sale; packaging is part of that moment
  • Brand recognition — consistent colors, logo and materials build familiarity
  • Protection — right bags prevent damage, dust and moisture during transport
  • Compliance — care labels and composition labels are legally required in most markets

1. Hangtags — Your Brand's Handshake

The hangtag carries price, size, brand story, and sometimes QR codes linking to your online store. Choices: paper (art, kraft, cotton), PVC for waterproof needs, or fabric tags for premium lines. Finishes like foil stamping, embossing and matte lamination elevate perceived value dramatically.

3. Garment Bags — Protection + Presentation

  • OPP / PE poly bags — clear, economical, standard for retail and export; printable for branding
  • Ziplock / self-seal bags — reusable, popular for online orders
  • Paper bags — kraft or coated, premium feel, eco-friendly image, good for boutique retail
  • Garment covers — for suits, coats and delicate items

4. Eco-Friendly Options (Growing Demand)

Many markets — especially the EU — are pushing for reduced plastic. Options: recycled kraft paper bags, biodegradable films, soy-based inks, and cotton or paper strings instead of plastic loops. Offering eco options positions your brand ahead of regulation and impresses conscious buyers.

5. Ordering as a System — Not Piecemeal

The most efficient approach is to order all components from one supplier:

  1. Design once — a consistent visual system across tags, labels and bags
  2. Order together — one shipment, one quality standard, one contact
  3. Save on freight — consolidated shipping lowers unit costs
