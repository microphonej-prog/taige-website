How an order runs

From artwork to a box of tags

Six steps, with one hard copy approval before the press runs.

Artwork check

Bleed, safe margin, minimum type size and foil line weight are checked before plates are ordered. Fixing this on screen is free.

Spec and quotation

Stock, size, colours, finishes and attachment fixed, then costed at your quantity bands.

Hard copy proof

3 working days for standard work. For foil or emboss you approve the actual finish, not a screen simulation.

Proof approval

Colour, stock and finish signed off. Plates and dies are made only after this point.

Print and finish

7–15 working days: print, laminate, foil or emboss, die-cut, punch, string.

Inspection & packing

Colour against the approved proof, die-cut accuracy, hole position, counted and packed per design.

Quality control

What gets checked before the carton closes

  • Colour against the approved proof — under the same light, not by eye on a different day
  • Lamination adhesion — no lifting at edges or over foil areas
  • Foil coverage — solid block, no pinholes, registration to the print
  • Die-cut accuracy — shape, corner radius and hole position within tolerance
  • Stock weight — the board delivered matches the board quoted
  • Count and packing — pieces per design and per carton, marked with the style number you specify
FAQ

Questions buyers ask before ordering

What is the minimum order for custom hang tags?

1,000 pieces per design, with a minimum order value that applies to small runs. That is deliberately low because a brand usually needs several tag designs per season — four designs at 1,000 each is a normal first order, not an exception.

How fast can I get hang tag samples?

Three working days from confirmed artwork for a standard specification: 350gsm art card, four-colour print, matt lamination. Foil, emboss and die-cut samples take longer because plates and dies have to be made, and a hard copy proof is worth the wait for those finishes.

Which paper should I use for a hang tag?

350gsm coated art card is the standard for retail hang tags: stiff enough to hang straight, smooth enough for fine print and small type. Kraft reads more natural but takes colour differently, white card suits heavy foil blocking, and 400gsm or thicker is worth it when the tag carries a lot of copy or hangs from a heavy garment.

What does hot foil add to the cost?

Two things: a per-piece surcharge for the foil pass, and a one-off foil block per design. The block is what makes small quantities look expensive — it is a fixed cost spread over the run, so at 1,000 pieces it is a noticeable share of the unit price and at 30,000 it is almost nothing.

Can you print variable data such as a barcode or serial number?

Yes, variable data — barcodes, serial numbers, one-tag-one-code — is quoted as a separate pass, and the data file has to be supplied in the format you want printed. Tell us at enquiry stage: it changes the plate and the production route.

Do you supply the string, tag pins and safety pins?

Yes, attachment is quoted as part of the tag order: cotton or polyester string with a barb, tag pins, or elastic loops. Colour and length are chosen with the sample, because the attachment is what the customer actually handles first.
