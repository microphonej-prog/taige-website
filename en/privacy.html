Privacy Policy

Privacy policy of Dongguan Taige Packaging Products Co., Ltd.: what personal data we collect from enquiries and orders, how we use and protect it, and your rights under the GDPR.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how Dongguan Taige Packaging Products Co., Ltd. (brand TAIGE, "we") handles personal data on the taigetag.com website and in the course of enquiries, quotations and orders.

1. Data controller and contact

Controller: Dongguan Taige Packaging Products Co., Ltd., Room 1042, Building 2, Liansheng Plaza, 143 Liansheng Road, Humen Town, Dongguan, Guangdong, China. For any data-protection matter write to info@taigetag.com or call +86 131 2811 8931.

2. What data we collect

Data you provide: name, company, job title, email address, phone or WeChat, country and delivery address, artwork and spec sheets, sample and order details, and the content of your messages. Without this data we cannot quote or produce your order.

Data collected automatically: aggregate visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics — no cookies, no individual profiling, no cross-site tracking) and server logs kept by our host GitHub Pages under its own policy (IP address, browser type, request time).

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • Replying to enquiries, issuing quotations, recommending materials and sampling (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b): steps prior to entering a contract).
  • Performing orders: production, inspection, logistics and customs (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Keeping commercial records and meeting accounting and tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Improving our website content and product information and preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f): legitimate interests). We do not carry out automated decision-making or advertising profiling.

4. Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies and runs no retargeting. Your browser stores only a single language preference locally, which you can delete at any time without affecting the site.

5. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell personal data. We share only the minimum necessary with freight carriers, customs brokers and partner factories to fulfil an order, and require them to use it for that purpose only. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (United States) and email is handled by Zoho Mail, so your data may be transferred outside China; we keep such transfers minimal and contractually limited.

6. How long we keep data

Enquiries and quotations are usually kept for 24 months; sample records that did not lead to an order for 12 months; order, invoice and quality records for the period required by Chinese accounting and tax law (generally 10 years). After that they are deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

Under applicable law, including the GDPR, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, ask us to restrict or object to processing, request data portability, and withdraw consent. Write to info@taigetag.com to exercise these rights; we reply within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

8. Children

This website addresses business customers and does not knowingly collect personal data of children under 16. If you believe we hold such data, tell us and we will delete it.

9. Security and changes

Enquiry and order data is accessible only to colleagues involved in that business and is transmitted over encrypted connections. When this policy changes we update the "last updated" date on this page; material changes affecting your rights are announced on the home page.
