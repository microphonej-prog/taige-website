About TAGE

A manufacturer focused on garment trims, packaging and printing.

Company Profile

Dongguan Tage Packaging Products Co., Ltd. is located in Dongguan, Guangdong, China, specializing in garment hang tags, woven labels, care labels, packaging bags, eco paper bags and brochures. We provide one-stop service from design and sampling to mass production for apparel brands, traders and sourcing agents worldwide.

We adhere to the principle of “quality first, delivery on time”, with a complete quality control process from raw material inspection to final shipment.

Our Strengths

  • All six categories in one factory — consistent quality and delivery
  • Fast sampling, fully verified before mass production
  • Experienced with international orders and export procedures
  • OEM/ODM and sample-based customization supported

How We Work

  • 1. Inquiry: send artwork, sample or requirements
  • 2. Quotation within 24 hours
  • 3. Sampling: approve the sample
  • 4. Production with QC
  • 5. Shipment: logistics and documents

Quality Control

5-Step QC, Traceable Batches

Complete quality control from raw materials to shipment.

Incoming Inspection

Raw materials (paper, tape, film) sampled and rejected if below standard.

First-Article Check

First article checked against the approved sample before mass production.

In-Process QC

Periodic patrol checks on color, size and print during production.

Final Inspection

AQL sampling before shipment; quantity and packing verified.

Pre-Shipment Review

Triple check of packing list, marks and documents before release.
