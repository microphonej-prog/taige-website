Hang Tag MOQ & Cost: Spend Your Budget Wisely

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 19, 2026

Hang tag MOQ and cost are among the first questions clothing brands ask before ordering. Why do hangtags have a minimum order quantity? Why does unit price drop so much with volume? Hang tag costing is no mystery — it comes down to tooling, printing, finishing and quantity. This article breaks down every part of hang tag MOQ and pricing.

A note: this article avoids fixed prices — unit costs shift with exchange rates, paper markets and finishing combinations. Instead, we give you a framework: know where the money goes, and you can negotiate a fair price.

1. What Determines Hang Tag Cost

A hangtag's cost splits into four parts: tooling (die, printing plates, foil blocks), materials (paper, string, lamination), processing (printing, die-cutting, stringing, binding) and labor & waste. The smaller the order, the more the first two cost per tag — that is exactly why MOQs exist.

2. Where the MOQ Comes From

  • Tooling and setup: dies, plates and foil blocks are one-off costs that tiny orders cannot absorb
  • Material minimums: paper is bought by ream/ton and cord by roll — suppliers must avoid waste
  • Machine efficiency: plate changes and color matching take time — tiny batches are not economical
  • Flexible suppliers offer lower trial thresholds: hangtags from 2,000–3,000 pcs, woven labels 1,000+, care labels from 300–500 pcs

3. Quantity Tiers and Unit Price

Hang tag quotes usually come in tiers: for example 3,000, 5,000, 10,000 and 20,000 pcs. The biggest drop happens once tooling is fully amortized — typically above 10,000 pcs the unit price settles into a stable low band; beyond that, savings slow down. For most brands, ordering one season's or one style's quantity at once beats frequent reorders.

5. Five Ways to Cut Hang Tag Costs

  • Standardize sizes and layouts so several styles share one die
  • Combine orders: produce several styles in one run and share tooling and setup
  • Use different paper for cover and inner pages — keep the feel, cut the cost
  • Concentrate hero finishes: foil on the logo only is far cheaper than full coverage
  • Have your supplier pre-check files to avoid rework on bleed, colors or fonts

6. Trial Orders & Reorders: Low Risk, Lower Cost

For a new supplier, a trial order before scaling up is standard practice: use 2,000–3,000 hangtags to verify paper feel, print color and lead time, then commit to volume. On reorders, keep the specs identical — the same die and paper mean consistent quality and may waive part of the tooling cost.

💡 Tip: Do not compare unit prices alone. Add sampling fees, freight, lead time and reorder consistency — that is the true total cost, and the cheapest quote is often not the cheapest overall.比价不要只看单价。把打样费、运费、交期与返单品质算进去，才是真正的总成本——低价供应商这几项往往并不便宜。
