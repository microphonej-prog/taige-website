Hang Tag vs Sticker: Differences, Cost & Best Use

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 20, 2026

Hang tags and stickers are the two most common information carriers in garment trims. Both can deliver brand, size and price messages, but they differ greatly in feel, cost and usage. Choosing wrong makes a brand look cheap; choosing right multiplies the effect. This guide compares them by difference, use case and cost so you can decide when a hang tag is right and when a sticker is.

2. Where Hang Tags Shine

  • Brand image — heavy paper, foil and die-cut shapes build a premium feel——厚纸、烫金、异形模切能撑起高端质感
  • Information capacity — brand stories, fabric details and QR traceability all fit——品牌故事、面料介绍、二维码溯源都放得下
  • Unboxing experience — the hang tag is the first thing customers see, part of the unboxing ritual——顾客第一眼看到的就是吊牌，是「开箱仪式」的一部分
  • Keepsake value — many consumers keep a beautiful hang tag, giving ongoing brand exposure——不少消费者会保留好看的吊牌，等于持续的品牌曝光

3. Where Stickers Shine

  • Sizes and prices — warehouse tagging and in-store repricing at negligible cost——仓库贴标、门店改价，成本几乎可以忽略
  • Promotions — seasonal and limited-edition stickers are quick and flexible——换季贴纸、限量贴纸，灵活快速
  • Logistics & anti-counterfeiting — barcode and security stickers integrate with tracking systems——条码、防伪码贴纸可配合追溯系统
  • Inner packaging sealing — the standard companion to poly bags and seals——封口袋、密封条的常规搭档

4. The Combo: Brand with Hang Tags, Function with Stickers

The most cost-effective approach in apparel is a dual track: hang tags carry the brand image and core messages, while stickers handle variable details like size, price and promos. You keep the premium feel and stay flexible.

5. Cost & MOQ Thinking

Because of paper weight and finishing, a hang tag costs more per piece than a sticker. But hang tags can be trialed at 2,000-3,000 pcs, while sticker MOQs are even lower. Exact pricing depends on material, ink colors and finishing — sample first, then order per season in bulk.

💡 Tip: For e-commerce, use hang tags for branding and stickers for thank-you or after-sales cards. For retail stores, let hang tags carry price and promo info while stickers handle stock management.如果您的主营渠道是电商，建议吊牌做品牌、贴纸做售后卡片或感谢贴；如果是门店渠道，吊牌承担价格与促销信息，贴纸则用于库存管理。
