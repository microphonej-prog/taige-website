Garment Poly Bag Types: Choosing Between OPP, PE, CPE, Zipper and Anti-Static

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 16, 2026

Garment poly bags may look simple, but they are the most important protective layer between the factory and the consumer: they keep garments dust-free, dry and scratch-free, and shape the unboxing experience. Materials like OPP, PE and CPE combine with flat, zipper and garment bag styles into many options — the wrong choice means brittle bags, torn corners or a cheap feel. This guide breaks down garment poly bag materials and styles, with a comparison chart and selection advice.

1. The Three Main Materials: OPP, PE & CPE

OPP (oriented polypropylene) is crystal clear and crisp, giving folded tops a structured, display-ready look with a satisfying snap; the drawback is brittleness — it tears easily when folded or in cold weather, so it suits shirts and T-shirts that are not repeatedly bent. PE (polyethylene) is flexible, tough and hard to tear, the most economical all-round choice, though slightly less transparent. CPE (cast polyethylene) upgrades PE: softer, clearer and drapier, it hugs the garment without bulging and feels premium — the mainstream choice for branded apparel packaging today.

2. Common Styles: Flat, Zipper & Garment Bags

  • Flat bags — the basic style, sealed on three sides with an open end; the best value, fitting most standard folded garments——最基础的袋型，三边封口一边开口，性价比最高，适合标准折叠包装的绝大多数服装
  • Zipper bags — a resealable closure lets customers open and reuse the bag; ideal for accessories, multi-piece sets and e-commerce retail, at a slightly higher cost——带可重复开合的封口条，消费者可反复取用，适合配饰、多件套与电商零售场景，成本略高
  • Garment bags — long oversized bags for suits, coats and dresses shipped on hangers, protecting against dust and creasing, with hanger holes or zippers available——长条大袋，用于西装、大衣、连衣裙等挂装运输，防尘防皱，可带挂衣孔或拉链

3. Special Needs: Anti-Static & Moisture-Barrier Bags

Anti-static bags contain antistatic additives or coatings that dissipate surface static quickly, resisting dust and lint pickup — ideal for wool, cashmere and down garments, plus gift-box scenarios where apparel ships alongside electronics. Moisture-barrier/foil bags block moisture vapor and oxygen, suiting long sea-freight cycles, humid markets or humidity-sensitive trims. Whether you need special bags depends on your market and shipping mode.

5. Selection Advice & Customization Tips

  • Choose the material first: OPP for display clarity, CPE for feel and toughness, PE when cost leads
  • Thickness (micron/gauges) drives stiffness and puncture resistance: thick bags for heavy garments, thin bags for light layers — avoid oversized bags that waste material
  • Printing and labeling: bags can carry brand logos, eco marks or warning texts, or take adhesive labels; more colors mean higher unit cost
  • Eco needs: recycled-content, biodegradable or down-gauged bags are available; the EU has clear compliance rules for plastic packaging — confirm before ordering
