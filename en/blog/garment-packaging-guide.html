Complete Guide to Garment Packaging: Bags, Tags and Labels

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 15, 2026

Whether you run a clothing brand, a garment factory, or an online store, packaging is part of your product. Customers see it before they see the garment — and increasingly, they judge you by it. This guide covers the full packaging ecosystem: hangtags, labels, and bags, and how to choose them as a system.

Why Garment Packaging Matters

  • First impression — 70% of purchase decisions are made at the point of sale; packaging is part of that moment——70%的购买决定发生在销售现场；包装是那一刻的一部分
  • Brand recognition — consistent colors, logo and materials build familiarity——一致的色彩、LOGO和材质建立熟悉感
  • Protection — right bags prevent damage, dust and moisture during transport——合适的包装袋在运输中防止损坏、灰尘和受潮
  • Compliance — care labels and composition labels are legally required in most markets——大多数市场法律要求洗水标和成分标签

1. Hangtags — Your Brand's Handshake

The hangtag carries price, size, brand story, and sometimes QR codes linking to your online store. Choices: paper (art, kraft, cotton), PVC for waterproof needs, or fabric tags for premium lines. Finishes like foil stamping, embossing and matte lamination elevate perceived value dramatically.

2. Woven & Printed Labels — The Permanent Mark

标签类型用途关键特点
主唛（织唛）领口品牌名高级质感、耐用
尺码唛S、M、L/数字尺码常用塔夫绸织唛
洗水标洗涤说明印刷以保证清晰
成分唛依法标注面料成分印刷或织造

3. Garment Bags — Protection + Presentation

  • OPP / PE poly bags — clear, economical, standard for retail and export; printable for branding——透明、经济，零售和出口标配；可印刷品牌图案
  • Ziplock / self-seal bags — reusable, popular for online orders——可重复使用，电商订单常用
  • Paper bags — kraft or coated, premium feel, eco-friendly image, good for boutique retail——牛皮纸或覆膜，质感高级、环保形象，适合精品店零售
  • Garment covers — for suits, coats and delicate items——用于西装、大衣和娇贵物品

4. Eco-Friendly Options (Growing Demand)

Many markets — especially the EU — are pushing for reduced plastic. Options: recycled kraft paper bags, biodegradable films, soy-based inks, and cotton or paper strings instead of plastic loops. Offering eco options positions your brand ahead of regulation and impresses conscious buyers.

5. Ordering as a System — Not Piecemeal

The most efficient approach is to order all components from one supplier:

  1. Design once — a consistent visual system across tags, labels and bags——吊牌、标签和包装袋统一的视觉体系
  2. Order together — one shipment, one quality standard, one contact——一次发货、一个质量标准、一个联系人
  3. Save on freight — consolidated shipping lowers unit costs——集中运输降低单位成本
