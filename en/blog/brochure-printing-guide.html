Brochure Printing Guide for Garment Trims: Paper, Finishing & Binding Explained

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 28, 2026

Buyers in the garment trims industry usually review a supplier's brochure before deciding to work together. A well-printed brochure directly showcases the craftsmanship of hang tags, woven labels, care labels and packaging bags, and is a key basis for brands to evaluate a supplier's professionalism. This guide covers the three core steps of brochure printing: paper, finishing and binding.

1. Why Brochure Print Quality Matters

Sample books, product catalogs and company profiles are the most used tools of a sales team. A brochure with blurry printing, obvious color deviation or flimsy paper makes customers doubt the factory's quality control; a refined brochure, on the contrary, noticeably lifts inquiry conversion rates.

2. Paper Stock: Coated Art Paper vs Matte Paper

材质特点适合内容
铜版纸表面光滑、色彩鲜艳、光泽感强产品实拍图、色彩丰富的目录
哑粉纸哑光不反光、手感细腻、文字清晰文字较多的公司简介、工艺说明
艺术纸有纹理、质感高级高端品牌形象册
牛皮纸自然环保质感环保主题宣传册

Weight also matters: 128-157gsm is common for inner pages, while 250-300gsm is recommended for covers. Paper that is too thin shows print-through; too thick raises cost and shipping weight.

3. Printing Techniques: 4-Color, Spot Color & Foil

  • 4-color process (CMYK) — ideal for photos and gradients, moderate cost, the mainstream choice for brochures——CMYK 叠印，适合照片与渐变色，成本适中，是宣传册的主流选择
  • Spot color (Pantone) — guarantees accurate, consistent brand logo colors——用 Pantone 专色保证 LOGO 颜色准确统一，品牌识别度高
  • Foil stamping (gold/silver) — adds metallic shine on covers or logos for a premium feel——在封面或 LOGO 上增加金属光泽，提升档次感
  • Spot UV — glossy highlights on matte surfaces to emphasize images and headings——哑面局部提亮，突出图片与标题

4. Binding: Saddle Stitch, Perfect Binding & Folds

装订方式适用页数特点
骑马钉8-40页成本低、可完全平摊，适合产品目录
胶装40页以上书脊平整、正式感强，适合公司简介
对折/三折页单页轻便易寄，适合单页宣传单
圈装任意页数可180度平摊，适合样品册

5. MOQ, Lead Time & Sampling Advice

Brochure MOQs depend on page count and finishing; always approve a digital or physical proof before bulk production to confirm colors and paper effect. Lead times are usually slightly longer than for small items like hang tags, so plan 2-3 weeks ahead for trade shows or client visits. Before sending files to a professional printer, check bleed, resolution and embedded fonts to avoid rework.

💡 Tip: keep your brochure consistent with your hang tags, paper bags and other materials — same brand colors and paper style — so the brand image stays coherent at trade shows and in sample rooms.宣传册最好与吊牌、纸袋等物料使用同一套 VI 色值与纸张风格，让品牌形象在展会和样品间里保持一致。
