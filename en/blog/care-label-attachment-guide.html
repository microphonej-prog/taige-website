Care Label Attachment Guide: Placement, Sewing Methods & Heat Transfer

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated September 1, 2026

Where and how your care label is attached directly affects compliance and wearing comfort. A bad position can irritate the skin, a bad stitch can fray or curl; for exported garments, care label placement is also governed by target-market regulations. This guide covers the common attachment methods, sewing positions and key considerations.

There is no single answer for care label attachment — it depends on the fabric, category, amount of content and compliance requirements. Start with the two main directions: sewing and heat transfer.

1. Attachment Methods at a Glance: Sewing & Heat Transfer

  • Sewn care labels — the most common option, suitable for cotton, linen, denim and other sewable fabrics; strong and removable——最通用，适合棉、麻、牛仔等可缝面料，牢固且可拆除
  • Heat transfer care labels — printed on a heat-seal film and pressed on, ideal for next-to-skin or seamless styles like underwear and sportswear——印在热熔膜上，熨压贴合，适合内衣、运动服等贴身或无痕款式
  • Woven ribbon care labels — durable and foldable into multiple layers, ideal for export orders with lots of content——耐磨、可折叠多层，适合内容较多的出口订单
  • Printed care labels — thin and soft, suitable for categories with high comfort requirements——轻薄柔软，适合对舒适度要求高的品类

3. Placement Advice by Garment Category

  • T-shirts / hoodies — left side seam, folded edge inward and label face outward for easy reading——左侧缝，折边朝内、标签主体朝外，便于阅读
  • Pants / jeans — inside the waistband, away from the high-friction center back area——腰头内侧，避开后腰中心摩擦区
  • Underwear — prefer heat transfer or seamless side-seam labels, avoiding any hard edges——优先热转印或侧缝无感标，避免任何硬边
  • Kids' wear — consider scratch-free labels or printed labels, placed away from the back of the neck——可选用防刮标签或印标，位置避开后颈

4. Sewing Methods & Durability

  • Flat seam — the label is sewn directly into the side seam; simple and low cost——标签直接缝入侧缝，工艺简单、成本低
  • Overlock (merrow) — the label and fabric are overlocked together for a cleaner edge——用包边机把标签与面料一起包缝，边缘更整齐
  • Folded seam — the label is folded and sewn in, leaving a flat face with no raw edges——把标签对折后缝入，正面平整、无毛边外露
  • The label itself must withstand washing and heat, but thread strength matters just as much; multi-layer folded labels wear most at the creases

5. Export Compliance & Readability

US FTC and EU regulations both require care label content to be clear, durable and easy for consumers to find. When content is extensive, multi-layer folded care labels work well, but the attachment position must keep the label fully readable once unfolded.

💡 Tip: confirm care label placement with your client in writing before bulk production for export orders; test-sew during sampling to verify flatness and readability.出口订单的洗水标位置建议在大货前与客户书面确认；打样时同步试缝，确认平整度与可读性。
