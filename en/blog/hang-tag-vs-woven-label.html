Hang Tags vs Woven Labels: Functions, Materials & How to Choose

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 26, 2026

Hang tags and woven labels are the two labels most often seen together on a garment, but their roles are completely different: the hang tag is a paper card hung outside, carrying brand display and product information; the woven label is sewn inside, carrying the main label, size and composition. Many new brands mix them up, causing duplicated or missing work in design and sourcing. This article explains the difference from function, material and cost to combining them.

1. Roles: Display vs Identification

The hang tag hangs outside the garment and uses paper, printing and finishing to present the brand logo, price, selling points and seasonal collection — it is the first touchpoint with shoppers and counts as marketing material. The woven label (main label, neck label, side-seam label) is sewn inside the garment and carries compliance information such as brand name, size, fiber composition, origin and care instructions, staying with the garment for its whole life — a functional label.

  • Hang tag info can change anytime (seasons, promos, new lines); a woven label is fixed once sewn in
  • Hang tags are usually removed after purchase; woven labels must survive washing and rubbing
  • Hang tags allow rich detail and free visuals; woven label graphics are limited by the weaving process

3. Cost and MOQ Differences

Hang tags follow the printing route: low setup cost, cheap unit price, ideal for frequent design changes and multiple lines at once. Woven labels need a jacquard setup with a higher upfront fee, but the unit cost drops quickly with quantity and the same layout can be reused for years. For small and mid-size brands, order hang tags in small frequent batches and woven labels once a year to get the best total cost.

4. How to Choose by Category

  • Fast fashion / basics — keep the hang tag simple, use polyester plain-weave labels for value——简化吊牌、控制印刷工艺，织唛以涤纶平纹为主、追求性价比
  • Mid-to-high-end womenswear / menswear — invest in hang tag design (cotton paper, foil, embossing) and satin woven labels——吊牌重设计（棉纸、烫金、压凹凸），织唛选缎面、光泽挺括
  • Kids wear — rounded hang tag corners, soft woven label materials, label placed outside the neckline for comfort——吊牌做圆角防刮手，织唛用柔软材质、标签缝在领口外侧更舒适
  • Sportswear / outdoor — durable or waterproof hang tag stock, polyester/nylon labels that resist sweat and washing——吊牌可选耐磨纸张或防水材质，织唛用涤纶/尼龙、耐汗耐洗
