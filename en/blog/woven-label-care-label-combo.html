Main Label + Care Label Combo: Order Woven & Care Labels Together

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 20, 2026

Almost every garment carries two labels: the main woven label at the collar or neckline, and the care label at the side seam or hem. The main label carries your brand; the care label carries compliance information — both are essential. Ordering main labels and care labels together in one combo is the choice of more and more clothing brands and factories. This guide explains the benefits, the process and the details to watch.

Buying separately means dealing with two suppliers, matching two color cards and juggling two timelines. A combo order needs just one conversation, one contract and one inspection — and the two labels are much easier to keep visually consistent.

1. Four Benefits of a Combo Order

  • Consistent quality — one supplier controls yarn, ink and process, keeping main label and care label colors and fonts aligned
  • Synchronized lead time — both labels are produced and shipped in the same batch, so production never waits on one label
  • Better cost — sampling and freight are combined, and combined quantities give more room to negotiate unit prices
  • Simpler communication — one artwork, one approval, and a single contact for revisions

2. Common Label Combinations

Main Label TypeCare Label TypeBest For
Satin main labelWoven tape care labelRegular garments such as shirts and dresses
Taffeta main labelCoated care labelFast fashion, high-volume production
Jacquard main labelTransfer care labelPremium lines focused on texture
Double-layer main labelWoven tape care labelDurable categories: denim, workwear

3. How a Combo Order Works

  • Send artwork or samples — confirm the material, size and folding (fold-over / tri-fold) of both labels
  • Sampling — samples usually arrive in 3-7 days; check fonts, care symbols and colors
  • Bulk production — typical lead time of 10-25 days depending on quantity and finishing
  • Final inspection — batch checks on size, color difference and print legibility

4. Details Often Overlooked

  • Composition and care symbols must be complete — the care label should list fiber content and care symbols (ISO 3758 is a good reference)
  • Agree on color tolerance early — satin and woven tape are different materials, so the same color shifts slightly between them
  • Folding affects size — a tri-fold care label is smaller and neater, but must match the sewing method
  • Flexible MOQs — woven labels usually start around 1,000 pcs and care labels from 300-500 pcs, so trial orders are easy
💡 Tip: Before a combo order, send one finished garment sample to the supplier so the pattern maker can size the labels to the actual seam — it avoids rework from mismatched label sizes.
