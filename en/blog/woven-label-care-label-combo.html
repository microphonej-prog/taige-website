Main Label + Care Label Combo: Order Woven & Care Labels Together

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 20, 2026

Almost every garment carries two labels: the main woven label at the collar or neckline, and the care label at the side seam or hem. The main label carries your brand; the care label carries compliance information — both are essential. Ordering main labels and care labels together in one combo is the choice of more and more clothing brands and factories. This guide explains the benefits, the process and the details to watch.

Buying separately means dealing with two suppliers, matching two color cards and juggling two timelines. A combo order needs just one conversation, one contract and one inspection — and the two labels are much easier to keep visually consistent.

1. Four Benefits of a Combo Order

  • Consistent quality — one supplier controls yarn, ink and process, keeping main label and care label colors and fonts aligned——同一供应商把控纱线、油墨与工艺，主唛和洗水标的颜色与字体风格保持一致
  • Synchronized lead time — both labels are produced and shipped in the same batch, so production never waits on one label——两样标签同批生产、同批出货，避免因等料导致停工
  • Better cost — sampling and freight are combined, and combined quantities give more room to negotiate unit prices——打样费与运费合并计算，数量合并后单价更有谈判空间
  • Simpler communication — one artwork, one approval, and a single contact for revisions——一份设计稿、一次确认，改版也只需对接一个人

3. How a Combo Order Works

  • Send artwork or samples — confirm the material, size and folding (fold-over / tri-fold) of both labels——确认主唛与洗水标的材质、尺寸与折叠方式（对折/三折）
  • Sampling — samples usually arrive in 3-7 days; check fonts, care symbols and colors——一般 3-7 天出样，确认字体、洗涤符号与颜色
  • Bulk production — typical lead time of 10-25 days depending on quantity and finishing——常规交期 10-25 天，视数量与工艺而定
  • Final inspection — batch checks on size, color difference and print legibility——逐批抽检尺寸、色差与印刷清晰度

4. Details Often Overlooked

  • Composition and care symbols must be complete — the care label should list fiber content and care symbols (ISO 3758 is a good reference)——洗水标要完整标注纤维成分与洗涤符号（可参考 ISO 3758）
  • Agree on color tolerance early — satin and woven tape are different materials, so the same color shifts slightly between them——缎面与织带材质不同，同一颜色在不同材质上会有轻微差异
  • Folding affects size — a tri-fold care label is smaller and neater, but must match the sewing method——三折洗水标更小更规整，但要与缝制方式匹配
  • Flexible MOQs — woven labels usually start around 1,000 pcs and care labels from 300-500 pcs, so trial orders are easy——织唛一般 1000 条起、洗水标 300-500 条起，试单门槛低
💡 Tip: Before a combo order, send one finished garment sample to the supplier so the pattern maker can size the labels to the actual seam — it avoids rework from mismatched label sizes.组合采购前，先寄一件成衣样品给供应商，让打样师傅按实际缝位尺寸设计标签，能避免「标签大小与缝位不匹配」的返工。
