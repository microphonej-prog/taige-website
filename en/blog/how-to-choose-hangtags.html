How to Choose the Right Hangtags for Your Clothing Brand

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 15, 2026

Your hangtag is often the first physical touchpoint a customer has with your clothing brand. It communicates quality, tells your story, and — done right — turns a first-time buyer into a repeat customer. But with so many materials, finishes and printing options available, how do you choose?

This guide walks you through every decision factor, from paper stock to printing techniques to budgeting, so you can order hangtags with confidence.

1. Start With Your Brand Positioning

Before thinking about paper or printing, ask yourself: what should the hangtag feel like?

  • Luxury brands — thick cotton or textured paper, soft-touch lamination, foil stamping, minimal design——厚棉纸或纹理纸、触感覆膜、烫金、极简设计
  • Mid-market brands — 300–350gsm art paper, matte lamination, full-color printing——300–350克铜版纸、哑膜、四色印刷
  • Fast fashion / e-commerce — economical 250gsm paper, bright colors, high volume at low cost——经济型250克纸、鲜艳色彩、低成本大批量
  • Sustainable brands — recycled kraft paper, soy-based inks, no lamination——再生牛皮纸、大豆油墨、不覆膜

3. Pick Printing & Finishing Techniques

  • Offset / digital printing — full color, most common; digital is great for small quantities——四色，最常见；数码适合小批量
  • Foil stamping (gold, silver, rose gold) — adds premium shine, ideal for logos——增添高级光泽，适合LOGO
  • Embossing / debossing — raised or recessed logo, elegant and tactile——凸起或凹陷的LOGO，优雅有质感
  • UV spot coating — glossy highlights on a matte card——哑面卡片上的亮光点缀
  • Lamination — matte or glossy film protects the print and adds feel——哑膜或亮膜保护印刷、提升手感

4. Size, Shape & Attachment

  • Standard sizes: 35×70mm, 40×90mm, 50×90mm, 54×90mm — or custom shapes (die-cut)
  • Attachment options: string (cotton/nylon), elastic band, plastic loop, or metal eyelet
  • Custom shapes (rounded corners, tag shapes, garment silhouettes) make the tag memorable

5. Know the MOQ & Cost Factors

Hangtag prices depend on: material, size, number of colors, finishing, and quantity. As a rule of thumb, 2,000–3,000 pcs is a good trial order for e-commerce brands. For bulk orders of 10,000+, unit prices drop significantly.

💡 Tip: Request samples before bulk ordering. A good manufacturer sends physical samples within a week — always check print quality and paper feel before committing.批量下单前务必先打样。好的制造商一周内寄出实物样品——下单前一定要确认印刷质量和纸张手感。

6. Work With a Manufacturer That Understands Apparel

Clothing accessories need a supplier who understands the apparel industry — the deadlines, the compliance requirements (care labels, composition), and the importance of consistent quality across reorders.
