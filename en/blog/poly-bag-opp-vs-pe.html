OPP vs PE Bags: Clarity, Feel & How to Choose

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 27, 2026

The two most common materials for garment packaging bags are OPP and PE. They both look like clear poly bags, yet feel and behave completely differently. Choose wrong and the pack looks puffy; choose worse and seals crack and the customer experience suffers. This article compares OPP and PE bags across five dimensions — material, clarity and feel, sealing, printing and price — with selection advice by garment category.

1. What Are OPP and PE?

OPP is biaxially oriented polypropylene film: stiff, highly transparent and crisp to the touch — ideal when the product needs to show off. PE is polyethylene film: soft, elastic, tear-resistant and heat-sealable — ideal for protective packing. Different raw materials mean clearly different performance and price, so understand the material before choosing.

3. Sealing & Opening Options

OPP bags are usually flat bags or self-adhesive bags, suited to hang tags, trims and single shirts, opened mainly by tearing; PE bags seal well by heat, making them right for zipper bags, garment bags and multi-piece packs that customers open and close repeatedly. For e-commerce shipping, PE zipper bags are usually the better choice: dust-proof, moisture-proof, reusable, and easy to repack returns.

4. Printing & Price Differences

OPP has a smooth surface that prints bright, crisp colors — great for brand patterns and logos; PE has low surface tension, so direct printing tends to peel, usually requiring corona treatment or switching to label application, which adds cost. As for price, OPP is generally a little cheaper than PE at the same spec, but thickness, opening style and printing all move the final unit price noticeably, so confirm with a sample quote.

5. Choosing by Garment Category

  • Hang tags & trims: flat OPP bags — clear display at low costOPP 平口袋，透明展示、成本低
  • Single tees & shirts: flat or zipper PE bags — soft, no fabric damagePE 平口袋或自封袋，柔软不伤面料
  • Suits & coats on hangers: PE garment bags — dust and moisture protectionPE 挂衣袋，防尘防潮
  • Multi-piece sets / e-commerce: PE zipper bags — reusable, dust-proofPE 自封袋，反复开合、防尘
  • Anti-static needs (tech fabrics): anti-static PE bags, custom made防静电 PE 袋，需单独定制

6. Eco & Cost Considerations

Both materials are available in biodegradable or recycled versions, but biodegradability and certifications such as EN 13432 require dedicated testing and push the cost up. Clarify the sustainability requirements of your export markets and retail channels first, then decide the material and thickness, so you never pay for specs you do not need.
