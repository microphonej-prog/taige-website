Woven Label Types Explained: Satin, Taffeta, Jacquard & Double-Layer

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 16, 2026

A woven label is the 'ID card' sewn into a neckline, side seam or hem — and the first detail shoppers use to judge garment quality. Unlike printed labels, woven labels are created directly from yarns, making them durable, wash-resistant and dimensional. There are several woven label types: taffeta, satin, jacquard and double-layer, each with distinct feel, cost and best uses.

1. How Woven Labels Are Made

Woven labels are formed by interlacing warp and weft yarns on a loom: taffeta weaves most tightly, satin uses long floats to create sheen, jacquard weaves complex multi-color patterns, and double-layer weaves two fabrics together then folds them over. The weave determines the look, feel and price — and what content each type presents best.

2. Taffeta: Thin, Fine & Budget-Friendly

Taffeta labels interlace warp and weft one-over-one, giving a tight, flat, thin structure that renders small text well. With a smooth hand feel, it is commonly used for inner labels, size tabs and small text areas. Taffeta is the lowest-cost woven label type with the most flexible MOQ — the workhorse choice for mass-market brands.

3. Satin: Glossy & Premium

Satin labels carry a fine, glossy sheen with a silky hand feel and vivid colors, reading as more 'premium' — the most common choice for main brand labels. They render logos and bilingual text crisply and suit mid-to-high-end garments. Note that satin's longer floats are less abrasion-resistant against rough fabrics or sharp edges, so pair it with a soft binding inside the neckline.

4. Jacquard: Rich Patterns & Dimensional Color

Jacquard labels weave multi-color weft yarns into complex patterns with crisp color boundaries, reproducing gradients, characters and scenery — ideal for streetwear and kids' brands that need strong visual impact. They cost more and take a bit longer to sample, but deliver the highest brand recognition.

5. Double-Layer: Clean Edges & Fold-Over

Double-layer labels are woven as two layers and folded over the neckline seam, leaving both sides smooth with no frayed edges and a more comfortable feel. They are the go-to solution for the 'tagless' trend and for premium next-to-skin garments.

Recommendations: choose satin for main labels, taffeta for small text and dense information, jacquard for complex logos, and double-layer for tagless comfort. Woven label MOQs generally start around 1,000 pcs — always sample first to confirm weave density, colors and hand feel.
