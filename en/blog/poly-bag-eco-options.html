How to Choose Eco-Friendly Garment Packaging Bags: Biodegradable, Recycled & PLA Options Explained

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 31, 2026

Eco-friendly garment packaging bags are moving from a nice-to-have to a must-have in Western markets. With regulations such as the EU Single-Use Plastics Directive taking effect, more buyers require apparel suppliers to offer biodegradable, recycled-content or recyclable packaging. Choosing the right material affects your eco image, your costs and your compliance.

This guide compares four mainstream eco options — biodegradable bags, recycled PE, PLA bio-based bags and kraft paper bags — and explains certifications, costs and sampling tips to balance sustainability with budget.

1. Why Clothing Brands Are Switching to Eco Packaging Bags

Three drivers are behind the shift: regulation — laws such as the EU SUPD restrict single-use plastic packaging; customer demand — major Western retailers now require eco packaging from suppliers; and brand image — eco packaging is a visible sustainability commitment. For apparel exporters, eco bags are no longer optional.

3. How to Read Eco Certifications and Marks

Look for third-party certifications: compostable bags usually carry EN 13432 (EU) or ASTM D6400 (US); recycled content can be verified under the Global Recycled Standard (GRS). For any degradability claim without certification, ask the supplier for a third-party test report before you commit.

💡 Tip: degradation depends heavily on the environment — industrial composting, home composting and landfill conditions work very differently. Check your target market's waste treatment and compliance requirements before ordering.降解性能与处理环境强相关——工业堆肥、家庭堆肥与填埋条件下的降解速度完全不同。下单前先确认目标市场的废弃物处理方式与合规要求。

4. Cost and MOQ

Eco bags usually cost more than standard PE: biodegradable options typically run 20–40% higher, PLA more still, depending on formulation, thickness and certification; recycled PE adds little. MOQs are quoted per carton or per roll, depending on size and thickness. Start with a small trial order to verify printing, seal strength and shipping performance before scaling up.

5. Printing and Customization Notes

  • Prefer water-based inks on eco bags to match your sustainability positioning
  • Some degradable formulations have low surface tension — always test ink adhesion before printing
  • Print recycling marks and material info on the bag to help end consumers sort waste correctly
  • Test seal strength and load capacity at sampling to avoid damage in transit
