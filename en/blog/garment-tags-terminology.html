Garment Tags Terminology: A Chinese-English Glossary for Sourcing

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 24, 2026

When talking to garment trims suppliers, terms like 'hang tag', 'woven label' and 'care label' differ across languages — one wrong word can derail a whole quote. This glossary organizes common terms and inquiry phrases by category, so you can communicate precisely whether you source in Chinese or English.

4. Useful Inquiry Phrases

  • Please quote FOB/CIF prices and payment terms.——Please quote FOB/CIF prices and payment terms.
  • What is the MOQ for this hang tag?——What is the MOQ for this hang tag?
  • How long does sampling take?——How long does sampling take?
  • Please send us 3 sets of physical samples for approval.——Please send us 3 sets of physical samples for approval.
  • What is the lead time for bulk production?——What is the lead time for bulk production?
  • Please quote per the attached artwork; colors match the Pantone numbers.——Please quote per the attached artwork; colors match the Pantone numbers.

5. Beyond Words: Specs to Confirm Before Ordering

Aligned vocabulary is only the first step — put the specs in the order to avoid disputes: dimensions (tag size, label width, care label size), material and weight/thickness, colors (Pantone or shade sample), finishing (printing method, foil, lamination), quantity and lead time, packing (pieces per bag, carton) and testing and certificates. Send this checklist to suppliers for more accurate quotes and fewer reworks.
