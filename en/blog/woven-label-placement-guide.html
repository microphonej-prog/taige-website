Woven Label Placement Guide: Neck Labels, Side Seam Labels & Category Choices

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated September 1, 2026

Woven label placement may seem like a small detail, but it affects brand image and wearing comfort. A crooked neck label or a curling side seam label drags down the perceived quality of the whole garment. This guide sorts out the common positions for main labels and side seam labels, folding methods and category-specific advice, so you get your woven labels right the first time.

There is no one-size-fits-all rule for woven label attachment, but the industry has well-established conventions. Start with an overview table, then refine by category.

1. Common Woven Label Positions

位置用途常见品类
后领中（主唛）品牌LOGO主展示位衬衫、T恤、外套
侧缝尺码标、品牌标、产地针织、卫衣、裤装
腰头/门襟品牌标识或尺码裤装、裙装
后袋/下摆辅助标识牛仔裤、工装

2. Neck Labels: Position & Folding

  • Center back neck is the classic position; side neck suits narrower necklines or more design-forward styles是最经典的领标位置；侧领适合领口较窄或设计感强的款式
  • Fold-over woven labels — folded before sewing to reduce friction against the back of the neck——织唛对折后缝入，减少对后颈的摩擦
  • Selvedge finishing — finished edges do not fray, so the neck label survives more washes——成品织边不散边，领标更耐洗
  • Neck label width must match the neckline structure; too wide and it flips out or curls

3. Side Seam Labels: Size & Functional Info

  • Keep the brand logo on the main label and put size, batch and origin on the side seam label — a clear division of roles
  • Keep the position consistent (usually the left side seam) so size information never varies
  • Side seam labels are small; when cutting wider woven labels down, check that the text stays legible

4. Placement Advice by Garment Category

  • Shirts / tops — center back neck main label + side seam size label——后领中主唛 + 侧缝尺码标
  • Pants / jeans — waistband main label (or leather patch) + side seam or back pocket size label——腰头主唛（或皮牌）+ 侧缝/后袋尺码标
  • Knitwear / sweaters — prefer side seam labels to avoid a heavy neck label pulling on the drape——以侧缝为主，避免厚重领标影响垂感
  • Kids' wear / underwear — choose soft satin woven labels or switch to printed labels, placed away from the back of the neck——选用柔软缎面织唛或直接改印标，位置避开后颈

5. Matching Size, Material & Position

位置推荐织唛说明
领标缎面、对折亲肤、柔软
侧缝标平纹/缎面窄幅轻薄、不卷边
腰头标平纹或提花耐磨、平整
尺码标平纹窄幅尺寸小、字迹清晰

6. Attachment Methods & Durability

Woven labels are mainly attached with flat or overlock stitching, and folded labels need reinforced stitching at the crease. Main labels and care labels are often sewn together at the same position to save steps and cost — see our article on main label and care label combos.

💡 Tip: before finalizing placement, ask your manufacturer for physical label samples and test-sew them to confirm flatness, feel and stitch strength before bulk production.确定织唛位置前，先向制造商要实物样唛试缝，确认平整度、手感与缝线牢度，再安排大货。
