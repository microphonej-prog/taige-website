Garment Export Packaging Requirements: Sea & Air Freight Packing

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 22, 2026

Garment export packaging is where damage rates run highest and attention runs lowest: a carton of damp shirts or a misprinted size mark can trigger a claim at the destination port. Garment export packaging requirements span four stages — inner packing, cartons, shipping marks and container loading — with different priorities for sea and air freight.

1. Inner Packing: Poly Bags & Moisture Control

  • Pack each garment individually in a poly bag to keep it clean and shelf-ready
  • Bags need ventilation holes and the choking warning 'Keep away from babies' — mandatory for US/EU exports
  • For sea freight on tropical routes or rainy-season shipments, add desiccant (silica gel or mineral) inside the cartons

2. Carton Specs & Packing

要点建议
纸箱材质出口用 5 层瓦楞纸箱（BC 楞），耐压抗潮
单箱毛重建议不超过 20-25kg，便于搬运、降低破损
装箱数量每箱数量统一并写进装箱单，方便查验与分拨
封箱加固井字打包带或缠绕膜加固，重箱加护角

3. Shipping Marks

The shipping mark is the first basis for sorting, customs clearance and claims at the destination. It should include at least: port of destination, order number, carton number (e.g. 1/50), gross/net weight, carton dimensions, style number and size breakdown. Add moisture and fragile warning icons on the sides.

💡 Tip: Print marks in English or the destination language, use barcodes or codes instead of plain text for the size breakdown to reduce sorting errors, and make sure the packing list matches the marks exactly.箱唛文字用目的国语言或英文，尺码分布建议用条形码/编号代替文字，减少分拨错误；装箱单与箱唛信息必须一一对应。

4. Palletizing & Container Loading

  • For sea freight, palletize on standard 1200×1000mm or 1200×800mm pallets, stretch-wrapped with corner protectors
  • Calculate volume before loading: a 20GP holds about 28 CBM and a 40HQ about 68 CBM — plan the layout with actual carton dimensions
  • Air freight bills on volumetric weight: for light bulky cargo, compress the packaging volume
  • Leave 2-3cm gaps filled with air bags to prevent shifting and collapse in transit

5. Export Inspection & Documents

Garment exports usually need no statutory inspection, but most buyers run a pre-shipment inspection (e.g. AQL sampling). Shipping documents typically include: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin (as required by the destination). QC focuses on: labels matching the goods, size counts matching the packing list, and no stains or damage.
