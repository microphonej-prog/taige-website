Woven vs Printed Label Cost: Price, MOQ & Saving Tips

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 27, 2026

Woven and printed labels are the two mainstream options for garment labels, and comparing their cost is one of the most common sourcing questions. Both are sewn into the garment, so why is the woven label clearly more expensive? And when does a printed label make better financial sense? This article breaks down cost structure, unit price, MOQ, volume tiers and saving tips, so your budget goes where it matters.

1. Cost Structure: Weaving vs Printing

The main cost of a woven label is the weaving itself: yarn, warping, loom time and edge finishing. Material and time costs are high, and pattern complexity, density and size directly push the unit price up. The main cost of a printed label (heat transfer) is printing: the design is printed onto a coated base material and slit into strips — a fast process with a low entry quantity. In short, the woven label costs more because of process time; the printed label saves money because of printing efficiency.

3. Five Factors Behind Woven Label Pricing

  • Size: width and length directly determine yarn usage and loom time; wider labels cost noticeably more宽度与长度直接决定用纱量与织机占用时间，宽唛比窄唛贵一截
  • Density & weave: satin and jacquard take more loom time than taffeta and cost more缎面、提花比平纹费工时，价格更高
  • Number of colors: more colors means higher thread and changeover costs颜色越多，换色与纱线成本越高
  • Edge finish: flat cut, hot cut and folded edges each have a different price平切、热切、折边工艺不同，价格不同
  • Quantity & reorders: MOQ and volume tiers set the unit price, and repeat orders can lock in better rates起订量与批量阶梯决定单价，长期复购还可锁定更优价格

4. When a Printed Label Saves You Money

  • Small trial orders: printed labels start at 300–500 pcs, keeping trial costs under control印唛起订低（300–500 条），试款成本可控
  • Many SKUs: with many styles and small quantities each, printed labels avoid heavy plate charges款式多、每款量少时，印唛不用分摊高额版费
  • Frequently changing info: when composition, origin or size runs change per batch, printed labels are cheap to update成分、产地、码段随批次变化，印唛改版成本低
  • Complex artwork: gradients and photographic effects are easier with printed labels渐变、照片效果用印唛更容易实现
  • Note: printed labels are less wash-resistant than woven ones; hot washing or heavy abrasion may crack them, so consider the use case印唛耐水洗性弱于织唛，高温洗涤或长期摩擦可能开裂，需评估使用场景

5. Practical Ways to Cut Label Costs

  • Combine orders with the same size and material to reach better volume tiers
  • Simplify the color count; one or two main colors is most economical
  • Use printed labels to validate styles and sales in the trial phase, then switch best-sellers to woven
  • Approve samples before ordering to avoid rework and waste
💡 Tip: use woven labels where the brand shows and printed labels where information lives — the most cost-effective combination for most clothing brands, spending budget where customers actually look.品牌展示位用织唛，信息位用印唛，是大多数服装品牌性价比最高的组合——把预算花在消费者看得见的地方。
