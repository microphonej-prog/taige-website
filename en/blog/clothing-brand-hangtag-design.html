Clothing Brand Hangtag Design: Size, Information & Visuals

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 22, 2026

A hangtag is the last brand impression before the customer touches the garment — it hangs at the collar and shapes the first judgment of quality. Clothing brand hangtag design is more than printing a logo: size, information and visuals must work together to carry the brand image while meeting the practical needs of shelf display and e-commerce shipping.

This article walks through every decision point — size, information structure, visual rules and finishing — so you can design your hangtag with confidence.

1. Start With the Size: Standard or Custom Die-Cut

Size determines information capacity and cost. Standard sizes — 35×70mm, 40×90mm, 50×90mm, 54×90mm — cover most garment categories with mature dies and fast lead times. For stronger recognition, choose a custom die-cut shape: rounded corners, garment silhouettes or tag-shaped designs — but this requires a new die, with a slightly higher MOQ and unit price.

2. Decide What Information Goes on the Tag

  • Brand identity — logo and brand name, the stars of the tag——LOGO、品牌名，是吊牌的第一主角
  • Product info — style number, size, color, price (optional)——款式/货号、尺码、颜色、价格（可选）
  • Brand story or selling point — a one-line slogan or material highlight that makes the tag speak——一句话slogan、材质亮点，让吊牌会说话
  • QR code — website, social account, authenticity check or video; remember to keep the quiet zone——官网、公众号、防伪查询或视频，注意留出静区

More information is not always better. Keep the front restrained, move secondary content to the back, and keep a clear hierarchy so customers find what they need at a glance.

3. Visual Design: Typography, Color & Whitespace

  • Typography — use no more than 2-3 typefaces and create contrast between headline and body——全吊牌不超过2-3种字体，标题与正文拉开对比
  • Color — lead with brand colors, keep to three color families or fewer, and avoid a cheap look——以品牌色为主，配色不超过3个色系，避免廉价感
  • Whitespace — whitespace is not wasted space but a source of premium feel, especially for high-end lines——留白不是浪费，而是高级感的来源，尤其适合高端线

4. Let Finishing Bring the Design to Life

工艺适合效果
烫金/烫银LOGO与品牌名金属光泽，精致醒目
压凸/压凹高端、触感型设计立体层次，低调高级
局部UV图案点缀哑面上的一点亮光
覆膜需要保护的印刷面防刮耐磨，提升手感

Prioritize your finishes: highlight one or two techniques per tag — too many compete for attention.

5. Design File Requirements

  • Use CMYK color mode and at least 300dpi resolution
  • Keep 3mm bleed on all sides and at least 5mm from text to edge
  • Convert fonts to outlines so no glyphs are missing in production
  • Put the die-cut line on a separate layer and label front and back clearly
💡 Tip: Screens and printed results differ. Always approve a physical sample before bulk production — especially for foil stamping and embossing, where the real result differs more from the design file than you might expect.屏幕显示与印刷成品会有色差。批量生产前一定要打样确认，尤其是烫金、压凹凸这类工艺效果，实物和设计稿的差异比想象中大。
