Hang Tag Printing Methods: A Complete Guide from Offset to Foil Stamping

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 16, 2026

Hang tag printing methods directly shape the first impression of your brand: the same logo printed by offset or foil stamped looks completely different in perceived value. With offset, digital, foil stamping, embossing and UV spot coating to choose from, the wrong choice wastes budget and cheapens your product. This guide explains the principles, best uses and combinations of the five main hang tag printing methods, with a comparison chart and sampling tips.

1. Offset & Digital Printing: The Foundation

Offset printing is the most common way to print hang tags: CMYK four-color process gives stable color reproduction and fine halftone detail, and unit costs drop sharply once plates are made, making it ideal for runs of a few thousand tags and up. Its weakness is that changing the design means new plates, so revisions are costly.

Digital printing needs no plates, so minimum runs are flexible; small batches, trial orders and variable data (such as serial numbers or QR codes) are easy. The downsides are a higher per-piece cost than large offset runs and slightly less predictable color on specialty papers. The two are not rivals: sample digitally first, then produce in offset — the most common path for brands.

2. Foil Stamping: A Metallic Touch for Your Logo

Foil stamping transfers metallic foil onto the paper surface with heat, adding a refined metallic sheen — one of the most premium-feeling hang tag printing methods. Gold, silver, rose gold, red gold and holographic foils are available, typically used for logos, brand names and border decoration. Foil works best on smooth stocks like art paper and cotton paper; on deeply textured papers such as kraft, adhesion and luster suffer, so always sample first.

3. Embossing & UV Spot Coating: Visible, Touchable Depth

  • Embossing / debossing — a die presses raised or recessed shapes into the paper, giving the logo tactile depth and shadow play; the signature finish of premium hang tags——用模具在纸张上压出凸起或凹陷的立体图形，LOGO 有了触感与光影变化，是高端吊牌的标志性工艺
  • UV spot coating — glossy varnish applied to selected areas over a matte surface creates a striking contrast, ideal for highlighting brand names, motifs or border lines——在哑膜或哑面纸张上局部涂布亮光油，形成亮暗对比，适合突出品牌名、图案或边框线条
  • Combinations — foil plus embossing creates a dimensional metallic logo; matte lamination plus UV spot coating keeps the card understated yet detailed, a favorite of luxury and premium brands——「烫金 + 压凸」做出立体金属 LOGO；「哑膜 + 局部 UV」让整张卡片低调而有细节，是奢侈与中高端品牌的常见搭配

Cost expectations: unit price depends on quantity, covered area and paper stock — final pricing depends on the process and volume. Two rules of thumb: the larger the foil area and the finer the lines, the higher the cost; and the more finishes you stack, the longer the lead time and the higher the waste rate. Lock in your hero finish first, then consider layering.

5. How to Brief Your Supplier

  • Send vector files (AI/PDF with outlined text); offset and foil both need crisp logos and proper bleed
  • Mark foil and UV areas with spot colors so finish placement is exact in production
  • Ask for finish samples: compare the same design in different finishes to verify adhesion, luster and emboss depth
