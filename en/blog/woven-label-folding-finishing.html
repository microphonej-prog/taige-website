Woven Label Folding & Finishing: From Flat and Hot Cut to Folded and Bound

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 28, 2026

The quality of a woven label depends not only on weaving — folding and finishing are just as critical. The same label material looks and behaves very differently when flat cut, hot cut, folded or mitred, directly affecting sewing efficiency and wearing comfort. This guide explains the common cutting and folding methods so you can choose the right process when ordering.

1. Why Finishing Determines Label Quality

Freshly woven labels have raw edges that fray and fuzz if left unfinished — once sewn into garments they look bad and may irritate the skin. Finishing gives labels clean, dimensionally stable edges that are easy to sew. Edge treatment is exactly what brands most often overlook when approving woven label samples.

2. Flat Cut vs Hot Cut: Two Basic Edge Cuts

切边方式工艺特点适用
平切冷刀裁切边缘平整，可能轻微起毛需折边或包边的织唛
热切热刀/超声波裁切边缘熔封不脱纱直接使用的织唛、缎面唛

Satin labels are usually hot cut, as satin yarns fray more easily; regular polyester taffeta labels can be flat cut, since the raw edge gets enclosed during folding.

3. Folding Methods: Folded, Mitred & Bound

  • Folded (center fold) — the label is folded in half and stitched at both ends; common for main labels, can double as care info——织唛对折后两端车缝，常见于主唛，可同时印洗水信息
  • Folded ends (both ends) — both ends are folded inward for a clean edge; good for labels with exposed edges——两端向中间折入，形成光边，适合露出边缘的织唛
  • Bound edge — stitching wraps the edge; strong and wash-resistant, for demanding brands——用缝线把边缘包住，坚固耐洗，适合要求高的品牌
  • Mitred (arrow fold) — ends are folded into an arrow shape for easy insertion into side seams——把织唛两端折成箭头形，便于穿入侧缝

4. Folding Options for Care & Main Labels

Care labels carry many lines of information, so they are often folded into sections to fit inside necklines or side seams. Common folds are half fold, tri-fold, four-fold and Z-fold; more folds mean a shorter label but slightly less convenient reading. Markets like the EU impose mandatory care label content, so fold design must balance full compliance information and wearing comfort.

5. Sewing Fit & Sampling Advice

Folding must match the sewing process: folded labels suit necklines, while mitred or double-folded labels fit side seams; care labels are usually pre-folded and sewn directly at the garment factory. When sampling, confirm four elements at once — material, edge cut, fold method and sewing position — to avoid rework in bulk production.

💡 Tip: if main labels and care labels are ordered together, use the same material and folding system so the garment factory can standardize sewing and you save on sourcing and management costs.若同一批订单同时做主唛和洗水标，建议用同一种材质与折法体系，方便服装厂统一车缝工序，也能降低采购与管理成本。
