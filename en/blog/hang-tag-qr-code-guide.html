Hang Tag QR Codes: Traceability, Anti-Counterfeiting & Marketing

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 21, 2026

A hang tag printed with a QR code is becoming a key bridge between your garment and the digital world. Shoppers scan to view traceability, verify authenticity, or land on your website and membership pages — all starting from one small QR code on the tag. This guide covers use cases, printing rules and cost factors for QR hang tags.

1. Three Main Use Cases for QR Hang Tags

  • Traceability — scan to view batch, factory info and QC reports; ideal for babywear, organic garments and premium lines that value transparency：扫码查看生产批次、工厂信息与质检报告，适合母婴、有机与高端品牌
  • Anti-counterfeiting — one unique code per item with limited checks helps premium brands fight fakes across authorized channels：一物一码加查询次数限制，帮助高端品牌与授权渠道打击仿冒
  • Marketing — scan to reach your website, membership sign-up, styling guides or social media, turning the physical tag into a traffic entry point：扫码直达官网、会员注册、穿搭教程或社交媒体，把实体吊牌变成流量入口

2. Printing Methods for QR Hang Tags

工艺适用特点
数码喷印小批量、一物一码每个码可不同，数量越大单价越低
胶印大批量同码单色或四色印刷，成本低

压凹凸高端品牌立体质感，凹凸过深会变形码点，需打样

3. Size & Printing Specifications

  • A minimum of 12×12mm is recommended; codes with more data scan more reliably at 15×15mm or larger
  • Leave a quiet zone of at least 2–3mm on all sides so cutting never damages the code
  • Dark code on a light background scans best; avoid white-on-color codes and busy backgrounds
  • Use error correction level M or H to tolerate 15–30% damage and still scan
  • Keep the code away from creases, eyelets and string holes

4. Static vs Dynamic QR Codes: Managing Your Data

A static code bakes a fixed URL into the pattern and cannot be changed after printing; a dynamic code redirects through a short link, so the destination can be swapped at any time — that is the biggest difference between QR hang tags and ordinary printing.

5. Cost & MOQ Factors

The QR code itself adds almost no cost — what really drives the price is the printing method, size and quantity. Digital printing suits trial orders of 2,000–3,000 tags; above 10,000 tags, offset becomes noticeably cheaper per unit.

💡 Tip: unique codes (one per tag) need a data system behind them — confirm your supplier's code management process and file specs before mass production.一物一码（每张吊牌一个独立码）需要数据系统配合，量产前请确认供应商的码点管理流程与文件规范。

6. Sampling & Testing Tips

  • Scan 3–5 times with different phones (iOS/Android) indoors and outdoors
  • Test contrast right after printing — especially for foil and embossing
  • Simulate storage and shipping: can the code still scan after scuffs or moisture?
  • Test how fast the landing page loads overseas; a global CDN is recommended for international buyers
