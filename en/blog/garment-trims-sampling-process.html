Garment Trims Sampling Process: Hang Tags, Woven Labels & Care Labels

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 26, 2026

Sampling is the most critical step before ordering garment trims: hang tags, woven labels, care labels and bags each have their own sampling flow, lead time and approval points. Sample well and bulk production passes on the first try; sample sloppily and you face rework, delays and cost overruns. Using hang tags, woven labels and care labels as examples, this article walks through every step of the garment trims sampling process and includes a ready-to-use approval checklist.

1. Before Sampling: Specify Everything Upfront

Providing complete specifications at inquiry time shortens sampling significantly: product type, size, material, printing or weaving process, colors (Pantone numbers preferred), quantity, attachment or sewing method, plus artwork. Vector files (AI/PDF) are best, with text outlined so missing fonts cannot change the layout.

  • For hang tags: note front/back, bleed and die-cut line; give vector outlines for custom shapes
  • For woven labels: state the weave (satin/taffeta/jacquard) and folding (fold-over, folded-end, double-fold)
  • For care labels: note the layout, wash-symbol graphics and any barcode area
  • Specify colors with Pantone numbers to avoid screen color disputes

3. Approval Checklist: Six Points

  • Size — measure the physical sample against the drawing; check the sewn size for labels——实物实测与图纸一致，吊牌量成品、织唛量缝制后的尺寸
  • Color — compare with the Pantone fan; judge spot and metallic colors on the physical item——与潘通色卡比对，专色与金属色以实物为准
  • Text — check composition, origin, care symbols and size word by word; one wrong letter can break compliance——成分、产地、洗护符号、尺码信息逐字核对，错一个字母都影响合规
  • Feel — does the paper weight, yarn thickness and softness match the brand positioning?——纸张克重、纱线粗细、柔软度是否符合品牌定位
  • Print/weave sharpness — are small type and fine lines complete, without breaks or blur?——小字号与细线条是否完整，有无断线、糊版
  • Finishing — foil position, emboss depth and lamination match the design——烫金位置、压凹凸深度、覆膜效果与设计一致

4. Common Mistakes and Cost-Saving Tips

  • Ordering without confirming material — a sample that misses the expected feel costs far more to rework than to sample——样品质感与预期不符，返工成本远超打样费
  • Checking only the front — the hang tag back and the inside of a folded label are often missed——吊牌背面信息、织唛折后内侧常被忽略
  • No written record of approval — put approved-sample production in the PO and use it as the bulk acceptance reference——下单注明按确认样品生产，作为大货验收基准
  • Sampling each item separately — sample hang tags, woven labels and care labels together for one shipment and one round of approval——吊牌、织唛、洗水标一起打样，一次寄样、统一确认，省运费也省时间
