Hang Tag Foil Stamping Guide: Colors, Materials & Design Tips

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 23, 2026

Foil stamped hang tags are one of the most direct ways for a clothing brand to look more premium: a logo or brand name that catches the light instantly communicates quality. Many mid-to-high-end brands choose foil stamping as their primary hang tag finish. This guide covers the process, foil colors, paper pairings and design file essentials, so you get it right the first time.

1. How Foil Stamping Works: Hot vs Cold Foil

Hot foil stamping is the most common method: a heated metal die presses metallized foil onto the paper surface, bonding the foil to the stock under pressure for a flat, durable, high-shine result. Hot foil suits small logos, text and fine lines with crisp edges.

Cold foil uses UV adhesive as the 'glue': the UV adhesive is printed first, then foil is applied and cured under UV light. It suits large areas or fine gradient designs, is faster and cheaper, but slightly less glossy and dimensional than hot foil. Most premium hang tag applications still use hot foil.

2. Choosing Foil Colors: Gold, Silver, Rose Gold & More

  • Gold (matte/bright) — the classic choice for heritage, luxury-lite and oriental brands; matte gold is understated, bright gold is striking——最经典，适合复古、轻奢与国风品牌；哑金低调内敛，亮金华丽抢眼
  • Silver — cool and modern, great for sporty, tech-driven or minimalist brands——冷色调、现代感强，适合运动、科技感或极简品牌
  • Rose gold — soft and warm, popular with womenswear, lingerie and accessories brands——柔和温暖，受女装、内衣与配饰品牌欢迎
  • Holographic/iridescent foil — color-shifting effects for streetwear, kids' wear and seasonal editions——随光线变色的炫彩效果，适合潮牌、童装与节日限定款

3. Paper Stocks That Work Best With Foil

Foil needs a smooth, even surface. Dense stocks like art paper and cotton paper give the best results; deeply textured papers (such as rough kraft) can cause poor adhesion, missed spots or rough edges. Always sample dark-colored papers first to confirm contrast.

4. Combining Foil With Printing & Finishing

  • Offset + foil — full-color artwork with a foil logo accent, the most common combination——彩色图案打底、LOGO 烫金点睛，最常见的组合
  • Foil + embossing — raised foil for a dimensional logo, a luxury brand classic——烫金后把图案压凸，金箔带立体感，奢侈品牌经典做法
  • Foil + spot UV — glossy highlights around the foil create layered contrast——烫金区外的局部亮光形成层次对比

5. Design File Essentials

The foil area should be supplied as a separate vector layer (AI or PDF). Keep strokes at least 0.3mm, body text 8pt or larger, and leave safe spacing between foil and printed areas to avoid registration issues.
