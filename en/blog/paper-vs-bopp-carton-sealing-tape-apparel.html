The Tape on Your Carton Is the Last Plastic in the Box

By TAGE Packaging · Updated November 27, 2026

A 60 × 40 × 40 cm export carton leaves the packing line sealed with about 1.5 m of tape. The poly bag inside has a spec sheet, a recycled content figure and a supplier certificate; the tape has a purchase order number and nothing else. At 48 mm width and 45 µm gauge a BOPP tape weighs roughly 2 g per running metre, so one carton carries about 3 g — and a million cartons carry three tonnes of material glued to the outside of recycled fibre.

Paper tape does not make the question disappear, it answers it differently: the seal becomes part of the paper stream instead of a contaminant in it. Whether that is worth the change depends on three things — how the tape behaves in a pulper, what your packaging declarations say about it, and what happens to line speed.

1. Fix the Requirement Before the Material

A carton seal has to hold through palletising, a container's humidity swing and a customer opening the box with a knife. Three numbers describe it: peel adhesion, shear (holding power) and initial tack. They are measured with ASTM D3330 for 180° peel and ASTM D3654 for shear, on samples conditioned to ISO 2233 standard atmosphere — 23 °C and 50 % relative humidity. Ask for those figures on the roll you actually buy, not on the tape family datasheet.

BOPP dominates because it is cheap and predictable: 40-50 µm film with either an acrylic adhesive (quiet on recycled board, weaker in heat) or hot-melt rubber (stronger initial tack, ages faster). Water-activated paper tape is a different animal: 70-90 g/m² paper with a starch or PVA adhesive that is wetted and pressed into the fibre of the carton. It is slower to apply, it does not lift in cold weather, and it cannot be reopened without leaving a mark.

2. Repulpability Is the Whole Argument

The technical case for paper tape is not paper good, plastic bad; it is that an uncoated, water-activated paper tape disperses in the pulper with the carton. Kraft paper tape with a hot-melt rubber adhesive often does not: the adhesive forms stickies that mills screen out, and the bale is downgraded. Reinforced paper tape with glass-fibre filaments is the strongest of the three and not repulpable at all, because the filaments survive the pulper.

Ask for evidence instead of an adjective. EN 13430 sets the requirements for packaging recoverable by material recycling; repulpability is tested with methods such as PTS-RH 021/97, and the 4evergreen Recyclability Evaluation Protocol gives a fibre-based grading you can compare across suppliers. One report covering the actual construction, adhesive included, is worth more than three certificates about the paper alone.

3. Where the Tape Shows Up on an Invoice

Two European instruments now price plastic packaging by weight. The UK Plastic Packaging Tax applies a rate per tonne to plastic packaging that contains less than 30 % recycled plastic; Germany requires declared packaging quantities under VerpackG through LUCID; Spain taxes non-reusable plastic packaging at EUR 0.45 per kilogram of non-recycled plastic under Ley 7/2022. PPWR (EU) 2025/40 sets the direction behind all of them: packaging placed on the EU market must be recyclable by 2030.

Run the arithmetic on your own volume before deciding. Three grams per carton is nothing; 300,000 cartons is 0.9 tonnes of plastic declared as packaging, and that declaration is what a customer's compliance team will ask you to support. Switching to paper removes the line — but only if the tape you chose is genuinely repulpable, which is why the test report is the document that makes the claim true.

4. What Changes on the Line

Paper tape needs a water dispenser, a clean cut and a flat surface. It does not tolerate a dusty carton or an operator in a hurry. Expect a slower first week and fewer cartons per roll. Temperature matters: the water has to penetrate the adhesive before it can freeze, so a warehouse at 5 °C behaves nothing like one at 20 °C, and the tape that sealed perfectly in June will lift in a January loading dock.

Cost lands in a different place too. Paper tape usually costs several times more per metre than BOPP, but a 70-90 g/m² water-activated tape is often run in a narrower width and its bond means fewer re-seals. The honest comparison is cost per sealed carton including labour and rework, not price per roll — and price per roll is the only number most buyers ever see.

5. Words You Can Print, and Words to Retire

Directive (EU) 2024/825 amends the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive: generic environmental claims such as eco-friendly, environmentally friendly or green are allowed only where recognised outstanding environmental performance can be demonstrated. Member states have to transpose it by 27 March 2026, and it applies from 27 September 2026. ISO 14021 already set the rule for self-declared claims: specific, substantiated, and about the attribute you actually changed.

For a carton seal that means: FSC-certified paper tape, repulpable according to PTS-RH 021/97 is defensible; plastic-free packaging is not, if the carton still holds a poly bag, a desiccant sachet or a laminated label. Write the claim around what the tape is, not around what the whole box wishes it were.

Three checks: does the tape's repulpability report name the adhesive; is tape weight inside the packaging data you send customers; and does the line have a water dispenser plus a removal instruction for whichever tape you keep?
