Care Label Standards & Washing Symbols Explained

By TAGE Packaging  ·  Updated August 16, 2026

The care label is one of the densest and most compliance-heavy trims on a garment. It tells consumers how to wash, dry and iron — and it directly affects whether your clothing can enter the EU or US market. Understanding care label standards and washing symbols is a must for every export-oriented apparel brand.

1. Why Care Label Standards Matter

When garments are exported to different countries, the content and symbols on the care label are governed by local regulations. Incorrect labeling can lead to delisting, fines or even recalls. Meanwhile, symbols that are blurry or that fade after washing cause consumer complaints when garments are ruined. A compliant, legible care label is both a legal requirement and a sign of brand responsibility.

2. The Main International Standards

Today's care label standards are built on ISO 3758, with each market adding its own local rules:

  • EU: EN ISO 3758 — washing symbols must follow the standard; generally mandatory for EU exportsEN ISO 3758，洗涤符号必须与标准一致，出口欧盟普遍强制执行
  • US: FTC Care Labeling Rule (16 CFR Part 423) — requires a permanent care label with fiber content and country of originFTC《护理标签规则》（16 CFR Part 423），要求缝入永久性护理标签，并标注纤维成分与产地
  • China: GB 5296.4 — instructions for use of consumer products, Part 4: textiles and apparelGB 5296.4《消费品使用说明 第4部分：纺织品和服装》
  • Japan: JIS L 0001 series — symbol system broadly aligned with ISOJIS L 0001 系列，符号体系与 ISO 基本一致

If one garment is exported to multiple markets, design the care label to the strictest standard to avoid rework.

3. The Five Groups of Washing Symbols

ISO 3758 groups washing symbols into five categories. Symbols must appear as a complete set in a fixed order; invented or simplified symbols are treated as non-compliant:

水洗盆形 + 30/40/60最高洗涤温度；下方横线表示缓和或轻柔程序
漂白三角形空心=允许氯漂；两道斜线=仅氧漂/非氯漂；打叉=不可漂白
干燥方形圆圈=滚筒烘干（点=温度档）；竖线=悬挂晾干；横线=平铺晾干；左上角斜线=阴干

4. Fiber Content & Origin Labeling

Both the US and EU are strict about composition labeling. The US FTC requires fiber generic names listed in descending order by weight (e.g. '80% cotton, 20% polyester'), plus country of origin and importer details. The EU also requires specific percentages for inseparable fibers. Wrong composition labels are a common reason for customs seizures.

5. Materials & Printing for Legible, Durable Symbols

Common care label materials include satin ribbon, coated ribbon and heat-transfer film. Ribbon labels withstand washing and feel soft, ideal for sewn-in main care labels; heat-transfer suits lightweight next-to-skin garments and resists wear. Whichever material you choose, symbols must stay sharp at small sizes, offer clear contrast, and survive repeated wash tests. A professional factory samples first, tests, then mass-produces.

💡 Tip: For the US market, meet both the FTC Care Labeling Rule and fiber-content regulations; for the EU, follow EN ISO 3758.
